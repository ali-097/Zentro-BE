# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](./XXXX-name.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed

## Context

What situation forces a decision? Constraints, pressures, what we know and don't.
Write this so someone with no history on the project understands the problem before
seeing the answer.

## Decision

What we are doing. Present tense, active voice: "We store money as integer minor units."

## Consequences

**Good:** what this buys us.

**Bad:** what it costs. Be honest — an ADR with no downsides wasn't a real decision.

**Neutral:** what changes without being better or worse.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the most valuable section: it prevents
someone re-proposing a rejected option in six months, and it tells them when the decision
should be revisited.
